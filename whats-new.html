New in 1.3

Nixie's Nightshade Pack

Nixie, an eleventh mermaid, comes in a pack of her own with a goth set in black and violet.

  • Nixie, with space buns in raven and violet.
  • The Nightshade set: the Violet Trim print for any top, the Violet Zigzag tail and Raven & Violet hair, with Black Orchid lipstick.
  • The Onyx Lionfish, a companion in black and violet, and 500 pearls.
  • One purchase, yours for good, and restored on a new phone. Find it from the link at the top of the pearl shop, or from Nixie in the wardrobe.

Also new in 1.3

A finished face, hair that follows her parting, and a bigger pearl pack

  • A finished face for every mermaid. Each one starts with soft arched brows and the Soft Glam makeup look, and the shading round her eyes is blended into her skin. Natural brows and no makeup are still a tap away.
  • Hair looks with streaks follow each mermaid's own hair and parting.
  • The Queen's Treasury, a pearl pack bigger than the Hoard: 8,000 pearls.

Since 1.2 · an invitation in 1.3

The Reef Runway

A weekly fashion show with other players. Each week has a theme. Dress for it, choose your pose, and send your look to the stage.

  • A theme every week, a new one for each week of the year. Dress for it in the wardrobe and send your look to the Runway.
  • An invitation each week, new in 1.3: the week's theme on the wardrobe's Runway button, and Lady Sapphira on the reef to show you the way.
  • 19 poses for the stage, with two that show her nails. Drag to turn her.
  • Rate other looks with one to five stars, and yours goes in. Each rating earns pearls, and Lady Sapphira has something to say about every one.
  • Save a look you love to your Outfits, or buy it with pearls.
  • Results every week in the Journal, with pearls to claim, and achievements for a streak on the podium.
  • No account to make. Other players see your look and a Runway name of three words that the game picks for you, and nothing else.

Beauty · since 1.1

Nails, makeup and brows

  • Nails in 8 shapes and 54 colors and finishes, from French tips to glitter.
  • 20 makeup looks, 10 brow shapes and freckles, beside 11 skin tones and lip and eye colors.
  • 65 pieces of jewelry: crowns, tiaras, necklaces, earrings, rings, bracelets and hair pieces, some earned on your travels.

Friends · since 1.1

Wishes, letters and visits

  • Grant your friends' small wishes, and each thanks you with a set of looks of their own.
  • Friends notice your new look and write you letters, and some drop by your cabin.
  • Befriend Old Sage, Lady Sapphira and the mermaids Isla and Lumi.

Wardrobe

Hair that flows, and more to wear

  • 65 hair colors and effects, with hair that flows as she swims.
  • 61 tails, from Classic Sea to Galaxy and Rainbow Scale, and fins in three layers and five shapes.
  • 20 tops in 17 colors and 44 prints, and a holiday set every month.
  • Furniture in front of the camera steps out of view while she dresses.

Decorating

Rooms of your own

  • Make a sunken ship's cabin yours with 69 pieces of furniture, each in four finishes.
  • Make a parlor, a galley, a bath, a dressing room or a study, and each earns a paint of its own.
  • Build walls as your home grows. A tap takes one down, and Doorway has its own button.

iPad · since 1.1

Pearl Tide plays on iPad

  • The whole sea on the bigger screen, with the words, panels and buttons sized for it.
  • Hold it upright or sideways.
