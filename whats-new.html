Beauty

Nails for your mermaid

Nails for your mermaid: 8 shapes and 54 colors and finishes. Plus makeup looks, brows, freckles and 43 new pieces of jewelry.What's New, line 1
  • Nails in 8 shapes and 54 colors and finishes, from French tips to glitter.
  • 20 makeup looks, 10 brow shapes and freckles, beside 11 skin tones and lip and eye colors.
  • 65 pieces of jewelry: crowns, tiaras, necklaces, earrings, rings, bracelets and hair pieces, some earned on your travels.

Friends

Wishes, letters and visits

Friends make small wishes and thank you with sets of their own. They notice your new look, write you letters, and some drop by your cabin.What's New, line 2
  • Grant your friends' small wishes, and each thanks you with a set of looks of their own.
  • Friends notice your new look and write you letters, and some drop by your cabin.
  • Befriend Old Sage, Lady Sapphira and the mermaids Isla and Lumi.

Story

Chapter Four: The Colors Return

A fourth chapter: The Colors Return.What's New, line 3
  • A gentle story, now in four chapters: the reef's colors are fading, and you can bring them back.
  • No timers, no danger, nothing to lose. Ten minutes or a whole evening, the sea is waiting.

Wardrobe

Hair that flows, and more to wear

Her hair flows as she swims, with new hair colors, tails and prints.What's New, line 5
  • 65 hair colors and effects, with hair that flows as she swims.

  • 20 tops in 17 colors and 44 prints.

Decorating

Rooms that earn their own paint

Make a parlor, galley, bath, dressing room or study to earn its own paint.What's New, line 6
  • Make a parlor, a galley, a bath, a dressing room or a study, and each earns a paint of its own.

  • Paint the walls, and build rooms of your own as your home grows.
