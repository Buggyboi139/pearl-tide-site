Privacy policy

Effective November 13, 2026

Pearl Tide is a single-player game with one optional online part, the Reef Runway. It has no ads and doesn't track you. You never make an account, and we never ask for your name, email or phone number. This policy describes the little information the game uses and why.

What stays on your device

Your progress, settings, the names you give your companions, and the photos you take in camera mode are saved on your device. We don't receive any of it, apart from a look you choose to enter in the Reef Runway (below). Deleting the app deletes it.

Purchases

If you buy pearls, Apple (the App Store) or Google (Google Play) handles the payment. We never see your payment details.

To confirm purchases and add the pearls to your game, Pearl Tide uses RevenueCat, a purchase-processing service that works on our behalf. RevenueCat receives:

  • what you bought (a pearl pack, early access to a holiday set, or a companion pack), its price and currency, and the purchase's transaction ID and date;
  • a random ID that the app creates on your device (not your name, email, or Apple or Google account);
  • basic information about the app and device, such as the app version, operating system version, and store country.

RevenueCat uses this only to process purchases. Its own privacy policy is at revenuecat.com/privacy.

The Reef Runway and pearl codes

The Reef Runway is a weekly fashion show where players enter a look and rate each other's. It's optional: nothing in this section happens unless you open the Runway or redeem a pearl code in Settings.

The first time you do, the game signs you in with a random ID. It isn't tied to your name, your email, or your Apple or Google account. Our server keeps, under that ID:

  • a Runway name of three words that the game picks for you (you can't type one);
  • the looks you enter: which of the game's items your mermaid wears, and her pose. No photos, and nothing you type;
  • the ratings you give, and any look you report or player you hide;
  • your results and the pearls they earn;
  • your game's language and time zone, and the version of the game you're playing;
  • pearl codes you've redeemed.

Other players see your Runway name and your look, and nothing else.

The server is run for us by Supabase, a hosting service that works on our behalf. Like any server, it logs the IP addresses of the devices that connect to it. Its own privacy policy is at supabase.com/privacy.

To delete all of this, open Settings in the game and choose Delete my Runway data. Your ID, name, looks, ratings and results are removed from the server right away. Your game on your phone isn't affected.

Photos

When you save a photo, the game asks for permission to add it to your photo library. It adds only the photos you choose to save, and it never reads your library. When you share a photo or your outfit, the game hands the picture to your phone's share sheet, and you choose where it goes; the game doesn't send it anywhere itself.

Reminders

If you allow notifications, the game schedules reminders on your device, such as when the tide's daily gift is ready or a holiday set is coming. Nothing is sent to a server to do this. You can turn reminders off in the game's Settings or in your device's settings.

Analytics and advertising

The game doesn't collect analytics or crash reports, shows no advertising, doesn't use your device's advertising identifier, and doesn't track you across other apps or websites. If this changes, we'll update this policy first.

Children

Pearl Tide isn't directed to children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example, the EU, the UK, or California), you may have the right to access or delete personal information about you. The only such information is the purchase record held by RevenueCat and, if you've used the Reef Runway, what's described above. You can delete your Runway data yourself in the game's Settings. To ask about any of it, or to have it deleted, write to support@pearltide.world.

This website

This website uses no cookies, analytics or trackers, and loads nothing from other sites. GitHub, which hosts it, keeps standard server logs.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date.
