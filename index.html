A tour

Eight things to do today

Press and hold to swim, slide to steer.

  1. 01

    Swim the reef

    Glide over the coral reef as schools of fish swim past. Look closely at a creature and it goes into your journal: fifty-one are waiting.

  2. 02

    Dress her your way

    Twenty tops in any color or print, sixty-one tails, makeup, brows and freckles, nails in eight shapes, and jewelry from pearl drops to a sapphire tiara. A holiday collection arrives every month.

  3. 03

    Make the cabin yours

    Your home is the captain's cabin of a sunken three-master. Furnish it square by square, recolor any piece, paint each room and build walls of your own. Make a parlor or a study, and the room earns a paint of its own.

  4. 04

    Make friends

    Isla tends her garden in the kelp forest and Lumi keeps the old songs. Bring gifts, grant their small wishes and grow each friendship heart by heart. Friends write you letters and drop by your cabin; at five hearts, Isla or Lumi swims with you.

  5. 05

    Strike a pose

    Twenty-two poses, twelve filters and twelve frames, and she always turns to face the camera. Share your photo the moment you take it.

  6. 06

    Ride a whale shark

    Call the Lavender Whale Shark and climb on its back. Companions swim at your side, fetch the pearls you swim past, and come to your hand to be petted.

  7. 07

    Swim anywhere

    Through the green light of the kelp forest, past the mermaid queen's statue, and up toward the bright surface. Nothing to beat, nowhere to be.

  8. 08

    Bring back the Heart of the Sea

    The reef's colors are fading. Wake the great whale asleep in the canyon, learn the queen's old song in the kelp forest, and sing it to the Heart of the Sea, deep in the Crystal Caverns. Then, in a fourth chapter, bring the color back to the places your friends love.
