Pearl Tide

A cozy mermaid life

Watch the trailer Coming soon to iPhone

Swim a sunlit sea as one of ten mermaids, styled your way. Make friends, help the creatures you meet, and make a sunken ship your home. No timers, no danger, no way to lose.

Thirty seconds under the sea

Ten mermaids

Each one her own

Coralie, Nerissa, Maren, Ondine, Delphine, Thalia, Calypso, Amaya, Serena and Aurelia: ten mermaids, each with her own face and her own hair. Every one is free. Choose one in the wardrobe's Mermaids tab and switch whenever you like.

The wardrobe

Dress her your way

Twenty tops in any color or print, dozens of tails, fins, hair colors and effects, eleven skin tones, and jewelry from pearl drops to a sapphire tiara. Save your favorite looks as outfits, and send your look to a friend with Share my outfit.

Holiday collections

Something new every month

Every month brings a holiday collection: a top print, a tail and a hair look, plus a pack of five companions in the holiday's colors. October's is Hallow's Tide, with the Little Bats print, the Spiderweb tail, Candy Corn Tips and a glowing Ghost Angelfish. Try a collection on a month early, and keep it for good.

Your home

A cabin of your own

Your home is the captain's cabin of a sunken three-master. Furnish it square by square, recolor any piece, paint each room and build walls of your own. The cabin grows as your story goes.

Friends

Friends all around the sea

Isla tends her garden in the kelp forest, and Lumi keeps the old songs at the queen's statue. Lady Sapphira and Old Sage live on the reef, and Auntie Ink, an octopus who makes things, waits outside your door. Talk, bring gifts, and grow each friendship heart by heart. At five hearts, Isla or Lumi swims with you.

The sea

A sea that's alive

Glide over the coral reef, through the green light of the kelp forest, and into the Crystal Caverns. Schools of fish swim past, fifty-one creatures wait for your journal, companions swim at your side, and the Lavender Whale Shark is yours to ride.

Camera mode

Strike a pose

Nineteen poses, ten angles, twelve filters and twelve frames. Bring your companion into the picture or go alone, then share your photo the moment you take it.

The story

Bring back the Heart of the Sea

The reef's colors are fading. Wake the great whale asleep in the canyon, learn the old songs in the kelp forest, and carry light to the Heart of the Sea, deep in the Crystal Caverns.
