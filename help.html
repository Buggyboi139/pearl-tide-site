How to play

Pearl Tide is a calm underwater adventure: no timer, no danger and no way to lose. You'll find all of this in the game too, under Settings > Guide.

Swimming

You begin on the coral reef. A canyon leads north to the kelp forest, and the story leads you somewhere deeper still.

Press and hold anywhere on the screen to swim; after a moment you start moving forward. While holding, slide your finger left or right to turn. Slide it up to climb and down to dive: the farther you slide, the steeper. Let go to level out and drift to a stop.

Pinch with two fingers to bring the camera closer or move it back. While you swim in the sea or the caverns, a Dash button shows by your left thumb: tap it for a burst of speed.

If you ever get stuck, open Settings and tap I'm stuck: you'll be moved to the nearest open water.

The compass and the map

The compass in the corner turns with you, and the glowing notch on its rim always points home. A pink heart shows someone who needs your help, and a gold star where your story leads. Pearls are small white dots, gifts little colored gems, and things ready to gather colored dots.

The star and the heart also show on the screen itself, with how far away they are. When one is out of view it waits at the edge of the screen with an arrow. Follow it: it leads you around the rocks.

Tap the compass to see the whole map: where you are, home, the places you know, your friends, and what grows. The legend says which is which; tap something that grows to see only where it grows. Tap a friend or a place on the map and a mark guides you there. Go home on the map takes you straight to your cabin.

Your story

Your first swim shows you pearls, keepsakes and your wardrobe. After that the sea is yours, and the story begins when you visit Auntie Ink outside your door.

The gold line under your pearl count says what to do next; tap it for a reminder. The Journal's Story page keeps every chapter so far. It begins with the reef's colors fading, and your friends have plenty to say about it as it goes. When the Heart of the Sea is lit, a fourth chapter begins, The Colors Return: keep visiting your friends, they have more to show you.

Pearls

Pearls rest on the seafloor, each with a soft beam of light rising from it. Now and then one is golden, and worth three. A new one appears somewhere else a little later.

You also earn pearls by helping sea creatures, finding keepsakes, meeting new creatures and growing friendships, and the tide leaves you a gift each day. Spend them on outfits, colors, jewelry, furniture and companions. Tap your pearl count to see every way to earn more, or to buy a pack of pearls.

Helping sea creatures

Now and then a creature needs your help: a pink heart on the compass and on the screen shows where. Swim close and tap Talk to hear what it needs, then I'll help, or Not now to come back later. Tap the line under your pearls to see how to help, or to give it up and help someone else instead.

Each one rewards you with pearls, and the first time you help each kind you also earn something special: a new top print, a hair color, a companion or extra pearls. On the reef each also gives you a glow shell; five of them wake the great whale asleep in the canyon, and the way north opens.

Your satchel

The Satchel, the bag by the compass, holds what you carry, your keepsakes, your pets and your photos. Gifts lie about the sea (sea glass, old gold coins, abalone shells and sunstars), and each part of the sea grows three things of its own, which sparkle when they're ready. Swim close to gather one; it grows back where you found it.

Friends

Lady Sapphira and Old Sage live on the reef, a curious creature watches the sunken stones, and Isla and Lumi are two mermaids of the kelp forest. Swim close and tap Talk, then choose what to talk about. Each friend has something of their own to share: Old Sage tells stories, Lumi sings, and Isla asks riddles, and challenges you to a race.

Bring them gifts: tap Give a gift at the end of a talk. Each loves one kind most, and their chats give hints. Each takes three gifts a day. Friendship grows in hearts, and at two and four hearts a friend gives you something of their own. At five hearts, Isla or Lumi will swim with you if you ask. She takes your companion's place and fetches what's near you, until you talk to her and let her go home, or choose a fish in Pets.

Wishes, letters and visits

Now and then a friend has a wish: something to wear, a photo, something for your home, or something to bring them. Ask when you talk. Each wish you grant earns a wish star, and stars earn pieces of that friend's own set for your wardrobe: a print, a hair color and a tail.

Friends notice when you change your look. They write to you, too: look in the shell letterbox beside your door and tap Read letter. Once a fish friend has three hearts, they may come to visit your cabin.

Auntie Ink

Auntie Ink, an octopus who makes things, lives on a ledge just outside your door. Bring her what grows in the sea and she'll make it into furniture for your home, and something your story will need.

The wardrobe

Inside your home, tap Wardrobe. Mermaids chooses who you are: ten mermaids, each with her own face and hair, all free. Then change your top and its color or print, your hair color, your tail and fins, and your jewelry. Beauty has your skin tone, freckles, lips, eyes, brows, makeup and nails. Drag sideways to turn around, and pinch to look closer.

Most colors and patterns cost pearls, and natural hair, skin, lip and eye colors are free. Some looks are earned instead, and say how: a compass rose for helping a creature, a heart for a friend, a star for a friend's wishes, a trophy for an achievement. Outfits keeps your favorite looks: tap Save outfit to keep everything you have on, and tap an outfit to wear it again. Each holiday collection is there as an outfit too. Share my outfit, at the top of the screen, makes a picture of your look to send.

Your home

Inside, tap Decorate. The view moves above your room, divided into squares. Tap Shop for furniture and My items to place pieces you own. Drag a piece to move it, and tap it to rotate it, recolor it or put it away. Small pieces can stand on tables and shelves. Paint gives each room its own color, and once the whale wakes, Build puts up walls of your own. Your room grows as your story goes.

Furniture comes in sets: the parlor, the galley, the bath, the dressing room and the study. Put enough of one set in a room and the room is named for it, with a wall paint of its own. Swim up to a mirror in your home and tap Look in the mirror: she preens in it until you tap Done.

Camera mode

Tap the camera by the compass. Drag to move around her and pinch to come closer. Choose a Pose and she turns to face the camera. Friend puts your companion in the photo beside you, or leaves you on your own. Soft background, the round button at the top, blurs what's behind her.

Try a Filter and a Frame, then tap the big button. Your photo comes up at once: tap Share to send it, or Save to keep it on your phone. All your photos are in the Satchel, under Photos.

The Reef Runway

The Reef Runway is a weekly fashion show with other players, and the one part of the game that needs the internet. Each week has a theme. Dress for it in the wardrobe and tap Submit to the Runway, then rate ten other looks, from one to five starfish, and yours goes in. Each rating earns pearls, and Lady Sapphira has something to say about every one.

Other players see your look and a Runway name of three words that the game picks for you, and nothing else. On any look, Save keeps it in your Outfits, and … has Report and Hide this player. The Journal's Runway tab has the week's theme, the ratings your look has had and, when the week ends, how it placed, with pearls to claim.

You never make an account. To remove everything the Runway keeps about you, open Settings, tap Delete beside Delete my Runway data, and tap again.

Companions

A companion swims beside you and fetches pearls, gifts and things that grow near you: watch it swim over, then what it found flies to you. Choose one in the Satchel under Pets. Some are free, some cost pearls, and some are earned. Holiday companions come five at a time in a companion pack. You can give each companion a name of its own. When you're still, tap your companion and it comes to your hand.

Riding the whale shark

The Lavender Whale Shark is in the Satchel under Pets > Mounts, for pearls. Once it's yours, a round button appears in the corner of the sea: tap Summon, then Ride. Riding, you swim half again as fast. Tap Hop off to let it go.

Holiday collections

Every month brings a holiday collection: a top print, a tail and a hair look. Each comes to the wardrobe for pearls on its holiday, and stays. A month before, you can try it on and get it early with early access. Each collection also has a pack of five companions. Find them all on the holiday card, which opens from a holiday piece in the wardrobe or the link at the top of the pearl shop.

Keepsakes and sea life

Ten keepsakes from the world above are hidden around the ocean, five on the reef and five in the kelp forest. They glow gold, and each one you find is carried home to a shelf in your room. Swim close to a creature you haven't met to add it to the Journal's Sea Life page.

Achievements

Achievements mark the things you do along the way, and each one earns a piece of jewelry. See them in the Journal.

Graphics, language and saving

Pearl Tide plays on iPhone and iPad. Graphics are on Auto to start: the game keeps itself smooth on your device, sharpening the picture when it can and easing off when it must, and remembers what suits it. Battery saver runs coolest and saves the most battery. Fixed holds one of six levels, from Low to Max, wherever you are.

The game speaks English, Spanish, French, Arabic, Hindi and Chinese (Simplified), and follows your phone's language unless you choose one under Language. Text size makes the words larger or smaller. You can also switch between vertical and horizontal screens (turn your device and a small button in the corner offers to), set sound effects and music separately, and turn reminders on or off.

Everything saves automatically on your device, photos included. Only what you do in the Reef Runway is kept online. Your iPhone and your iPad each keep a game of their own. Deleting the app deletes your progress and photos, so save the photos you love to your device first.
